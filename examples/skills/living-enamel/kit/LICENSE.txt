Living Enamel toolkit provenance

The Python renderer, character geometry, set drawings, procedural score and DSP
were created for the local Living Enamel pilot in November 2026, then adapted into
this reusable skill. This notice records provenance; it does not assert third-party
copyright ownership or grant a license over material whose ownership is unverified.
The repository/project owner's existing terms apply to the original toolkit.

Cinzel.ttf is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Keep
assets/fonts/OFL.txt with redistributed font files.

Python dependencies retain their own package licenses. Kokoro weights are downloaded
separately from the public kokoro-onnx release; they are not embedded in this skill.
Consult their upstream license and notices before redistributing those weights.
The model URLs and actual downloaded SHA-256 hashes are recorded by setup.

CC0 permits commercial use, modification and redistribution; attribution is

assets/sfx/manifest.json maps EVERY bundled audio file to its source URL,
author, license, source recording hash, processing/layers and bundled SHA-256.
assets/sfx/licenses/ preserves the pack's original license/readme where present
and exact primary source-page CC0 fields (inert text), URLs and page hashes,
verified 2026-11-14. Keep these notices and the manifest with the library.
The movement/places additions were verified 2026-11-15 against each primary
OpenGameArt page's own CC0 licence field. Their evidence files preserve cleaned
primary-page text, retrieval URLs and hashes; archive and individual source/bundle
hashes are in the manifest. No account was used. Hall, market and battle are
designed layers of those CC0 recordings, not literal location recordings.
No CC-BY/CC-BY-SA recordings from the researched crowd packs are bundled.
The effects were measured, not listened to; no subjective audio review is claimed.
No third-party image/video assets are bundled.
