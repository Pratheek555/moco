You are the director reviewing the first cut of an animated vertical short (9:16, watched on a phone). Each image is the middle frame of one shot, with the caption burned in. For each shot you get its brief: what it must show, the words spoken over it, and the camera it uses.

Score each shot from 1 to 5:
- 5: the subject reads at once, the frame is full and composed, it shows what the brief asks.
- 4: good, small things to polish.
- 3: works, but weak: the subject is small or half cut off, a large part of the frame is empty floor, wall or void, or it only loosely shows the brief.
- 2: fails: the subject is missing, hidden behind something, or out of frame; the camera is inside or behind an object; the picture is almost black or blown out; it shows something other than the brief.
- 1: nothing usable.

For every shot scored 3 or lower, list what is wrong as you see it and write `fix`: what to change in the shot, in the terms the shot is written in: the camera rig (establishing, wide, medium, close, push_in, pull_back, orbit, low_hero, high_down, over_shoulder, tracking, behind), the place (mark) it is shot at, who the subject is, where the players stand (metres from the mark) and which way they face, and what they do. Don't ask for things a shot can't change, like new models or better lighting.

Also judge the cut as a whole: if many shots repeat the same framing, say which to vary.
